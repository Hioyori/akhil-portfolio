01 — ABOUT ME

THE JOURNEY SO FAR.

I DON'T JUST EDIT VIDEOS. I CREATE EXPERIENCES.

Hi, I'm Akhil Kumar, a passionate filmmaker and creative artist with over 5 years of experience in the creative industry.

I specialize in video editing, 2D motion graphics, graphic design, and colour grading, and I also have knowledge of 2D animation.

I have worked with an in-house creative team and two advertising agencies in Kochi and Bangalore. Along with my professional experience, I also work as a part-time freelancer with clients based abroad, helping them bring their creative ideas to life.

I love telling stories through visuals and creating engaging content through videos, motion graphics, design, and colour. I'm always excited to explore new ideas, learn new techniques, and improve my creative skills.

My goal is to create meaningful visuals that connect with people and leave a lasting impression.
